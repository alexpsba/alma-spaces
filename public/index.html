05 / THE STORY BEHIND ALMA

It started
with a space.

Jessica’s journey into construction began unexpectedly, with a part-time position at a construction company. What started as a job quickly became a genuine passion for transforming spaces.

There was something deeply meaningful about watching walls become rooms, rooms become homes, and homes become part of someone’s story.

Along the way, Jessica discovered that what she valued most was the personal connection the work created. Every client brings a different vision, a different lifestyle, and a story waiting to be told.

“Many clients have become friends — and, in a small but meaningful way, I’ve had the privilege of becoming part of their story too.”
